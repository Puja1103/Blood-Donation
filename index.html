Blood Group Compatibility Quick Guide

Understanding which blood types can give and receive helps save precious time in emergencies.

Blood Type Can Donate Blood To Can Receive Blood From Special Note
O- Everyone (All Blood Types) O- only Universal Red Cell Donor
O+ O+, A+, B+, AB+ O+, O- Most commonly needed blood type
A+ A+, AB+ A+, A-, O+, O- Second most common blood type
A- A+, A-, AB+, AB- A-, O- Important for Rh-negative recipients

B- B+, B-, AB+, AB- B-, O- Rare and crucial blood type

Demonstration & Academic Project Disclaimer

Important Notice: This website is an educational demonstration project designed to showcase a front-end blood donation coordination workflow using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It does not replace certified healthcare services.

Actual donor eligibility, blood safety testing, cross-matching, and availability must always be officially verified through qualified medical personnel, registered hospitals, or government-authorized blood banks. In real-world medical emergencies, please immediately reach out to your nearest hospital emergency department or dial your regional emergency helpline (e.g. 108 / 112 / 104).
